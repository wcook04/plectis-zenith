# Zenith observed-system protocol

Zenith receives observations of a system at a declared cutoff t, freezes forecasts of later observations, and evaluates those forecasts when the exact target observations become available. The public System A example is independently generated by disclosed code and seed 17. It demonstrates the protocol; knowledge of its generator prevents treating it as a blind forecasting experiment.

Every numeric observation preserves target_id, value, unit, observed_at and available_at. The observation and its availability must precede the Lab cutoff. Acquisition time does not establish either clock. Missing records, failed attempts and uncertainty remain explicit. The six perspectives view the same system; agreement among correlated perspectives is not independent evidence.


## Clocks

Observed_at records when a quantity or event occurred. Available_at records when the observation could enter context. Cutoff t bounds both. Snapshot_time freezes the issue time; target_time_iso freezes a strictly later horizon. Evaluation as_of bounds the availability of truth. Every clock is timezone-aware.

The synthetic generator deliberately assigns occurrence and availability together. That authored simplification is disclosed and is not evidence that real observation clocks would coincide. Providers and model training cutoffs are separate sources of possible leakage.
