# Zenith observed-system protocol

Zenith receives observations of a system at a declared cutoff t, freezes forecasts of later observations, and evaluates those forecasts when the exact target observations become available. The public System A example is independently generated by disclosed code and seed 17. It demonstrates the protocol; knowledge of its generator prevents treating it as a blind forecasting experiment.

Every numeric observation preserves target_id, value, unit, observed_at and available_at. The observation and its availability must precede the Lab cutoff. Acquisition time does not establish either clock. Missing records, failed attempts and uncertainty remain explicit. The six perspectives view the same system; agreement among correlated perspectives is not independent evidence.


## Lab

Seed inventories coverage, clocks, units and missingness across all feeds. First-pass miners extract observations and uncertainty through their distinct perspective roles. Cross-correlation maps shared support and disagreement without converting association into causation. Orient constructs competing, grounded system hypotheses. Decide freezes CP1 evidence and the selected hypothesis or explicitly abstains.

Second-pass miners test that frozen hypothesis against their original observations, retaining contradictions. Cross-correlation verifies target support. Director freezes CP2 forecasts: target_id, snapshot_value, target_value, unit, snapshot_time, explicit target_time_iso, direction, invalidation and cited forecast_rationale. UP and DOWN compare quantities directly; finite zero and negative values are valid. Integrity audits the commitments rather than silently replacing them.

Lab cannot see later truth or Oracle explanations. Evidence identities remain stable through the graph. Model weights remain fixed. No admitted intermediate output establishes forecasting skill.
