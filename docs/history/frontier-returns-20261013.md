Historical research record. References to “current” or “next” describe this stage; see [today’s findings](../status.md).

## What each return changes

**01 — learning object.** Retain an independently grounded discriminating experiment alongside useful code. An authored 35-candidate search repairs the historical primary from 232/256 to 256/256, yet two additional immutable-payload conflicts still fail. The search location, grammar and 54 checks were supplied. Generative relations are accepted as a possible source of reusable supervision; autonomous law acquisition and its advantage over ordinary code plus tests are deferred. A passing invariance needs anchors and separating contrasts: constants and universal unresolved answers can otherwise evade it.

**02 — learning from errors.** Separate the search parent from the program selected for use, and make a repeated state require new evidence or a changed search. Five V6 saved repair requests and six proposed sources repeat identically. The rejected candidate fixes 40 development cases and loses four. The authored AST search repairs the same V5 defect, but its edit family and applicability were supplied. Tested source-edit contracts remain a hypothesis until acquired and reused by the model. Blindly transferring deduplication to a per-delivery target is rejected.

**03 — semantic discovery.** A measured continuation can reveal a missing history distinction; candidate disagreement and independent challenge serve different purposes. The finite-state learner acquires four states and binds SQLite `put` to a two-operation sequence, beyond renaming. All surviving bindings nevertheless agree on an incorrect near-match until an independent challenge refutes them. This supports executable source-response prediction and explicit ambiguity. A universal guarantee from candidate consensus, or an LLM discovery claim from this classical authored learner, is rejected.

**04 — active investigation.** Track which conclusions an evidence substitution supports and preserve the others as open obligations. The strict authored planner obtains attainable findings with less ordinary synthetic effort than a tuned runbook, but a fixed panel wins when costs change; validation payback against the runbook is 15,407 uses. Its quarantine and validity contracts are supplied. The exhaustive planner and autonomous substitution acquisition are deferred. Empirical probability zero cannot close an untried obligation, and synthetic effort is not API dollars or production savings.

**05 — compositional knowledge.** Share numerical code while retaining separate identity, unit and population bindings. Exact extraction saves 327 of 8,090 corpus AST nodes and preserves every historical error. Its strongest witness shows that projecting distinct events to endpoint sets before work aggregation can turn ratio 1.5 into 3.0. Reconcile identity and conflicts before discarding fields; separate behaviour-preserving refactoring from semantic repair. A general library framework is deferred until independent acquisition and later composition beat a competent utility repository.

**06 — scope and revision.** Revise source-conditioned permission to reuse without deleting every computation or unrelated conclusion. First compare simple global replacement: the fitted router scores 137/256 where the rejected program globally scores 150/256. The changing-source specimen saves checks but makes silent-change errors, ties an equivalent handwritten policy, and loses to fresh checking when checks are cheap. Source-local challenge and revision are accepted; elaborate routing and a universal empirical no-regression veto are not default choices. A checksum is custody evidence, not evidence that meaning persists.

**07 — world and feedback.** Let a host-executed computation provide limited behavioural feedback. The return uses real public SQL specimens, disposable copies and reversible physical changes; its authored rewrite is not a learned policy. Accept independent host observations, counterexample custody and cache/build/validation costs. A production optimisation result, general SQL-equivalence claim or result on the unacquired complete external dataset is rejected. Large-scale SQL optimisation remains an alternative domain rather than an additional mandatory framework.

**08 — decisive experiment.** Compare incremental state with an execution-enabled archive and a matched-budget fresh compiler on identical experience. These controls are accepted because they can choose the simpler system. The return's hypothetical large campaign and tariffs are planning arithmetic, not actual spending, and its fixture does not establish a memory advantage.

**09 — self-directed learning.** Prefer experiences that separate plausible computations, while anchoring usefulness outside the producing experience. The authored allocator does not establish cost advantage over external-error sampling, and a myopic stopping rule blocks a prerequisite chain. Retain these counterexamples and bounded exploration as research leads. Adaptive curriculum is deferred from the common-experience comparison so representation and acquisition are not changed together.

**10 — unifying theory.** Retain an executable observation model with binding, receipts and counterexamples; compile answers only to the extent the available observations identify them. The affine semantic catalogue, rank-based probe menu and deterministic compiler are not model-discovered structure or a general theory of intelligence.

## One mechanism and a test that can reject it

The common implementation uses the existing bounded executor, provider budget and source custody. Its retained state can be immutable predictor code, a small source-model record, receipt references, counterexamples and exact dependencies. No graph, universal ontology or parallel application is required. Model-proposed actions are executed by the host; actual observations are retained before interpretation. Missing support for one quantity need not erase an independently supported finding.

The acceptance recommendations conflict in one useful place. Return 02 can preserve a strict champion gate while exploring a stronger challenger; return 06 shows that preserving every empirical success can itself overgeneralise. The resolution is to separate search from use, retain regressions, and compare keep/global/conditional alternatives under the declared protocol. Independently warranted execution constraints differ from fallible empirical successes. Protected outcomes must not choose the policy or the best learner.

The native-source follow-on tests model-written predictors and model-proposed calibration actions, rather than relabelling the supplied prototypes as learning. Common observations, equal execution facilities and matched total construction allowances separate incremental organisation from information access. Fresh compilation and a competent executable archive remain serious controls. The frozen early predictor receives the final history; ties limit any claim that continued program revision was necessary. The current runner removes raw history as a control. Mismatched-source support, unaffected-source checks and recurrence remain recommended follow-on dependency tests; this campaign does not implement them. The proposed identification guard is conditional on a frozen native software family; configured sources are within-family replications, not separate semantic domains.

A result that only gains more observations supports useful evidence acquisition. A first proposal that already solves the task supports compilation. Repeated repair supports measured adaptation. Further-case gains attributable to retained computation, beyond equal-history controls and complete discovery costs, are the stronger claim still to earn. The campaign's actual source, pricing, attempts and outcomes belong in its separate source-bound receipt and the [measured status](../status.md), rather than being inferred from these returns.

The completed initial campaign found the stable-program explanation: its early and final incremental code tied on all six sources. The next screens put returns 02 and 06's search/use distinction to work: preserve the observations but permit cold reconstruction when edits repeat. The selected DeepSeek reconstruction then improved three failed programs from 23/288 to 188/288 on separate confirmation structures. A further candidate-pool comparison tests complementary model proposals under a development-only choice rule; a separate composition comparison tests return 05's learned dependency idea against the same code in a competent flat library. These are connected interventions on the same retained object, not ten separate subsystems or evidence that every return's stronger hypothesis holds.

The subsequent mixed-model pool confirmed at 279/288, but all winning programs were already retained. The stronger zero-inference follow-on selected from all 18 same-source historical proposals and reached 286/288 on a different panel after two fixed native probes per source. Its larger historical search budget is explicit. Candidate disagreement gave a superficially better score in the smaller inventory by avoiding a probe that exposed shared model misspecification. This reconnects returns 03, 06, 08 and 09: useful evidence can lower a flawed program's apparent coverage; archive selection and independent challenge must precede claims that an adaptive investigator is learning better.
