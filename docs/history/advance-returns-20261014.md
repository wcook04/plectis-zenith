# Fifteen advance returns: learn what to ask and retain what it reveals

Historical research record. References to “current” or “next” describe this stage; see [today’s findings](../status.md).

Zenith now has one executable **program, experiment and evidence loop**. A model writes an investigation policy, the host measures its questions against native software and the actual candidate program, and a repair can change any part of the ordinary program. Useful code and revealing tests remain available for later work. A graph can expose their relationships; it is an optional way to organise this process.

The fifteen returns strengthen this direction with bounded acquisition algorithms, native failures, stronger ordinary controls and proposed cleanup. We inspected their arguments and critical code, checked **762 manifest-listed payloads across fifteen archives**, and ran focused offline reproductions. The [custody and decision receipt](advance-returns-20261014.json) separates original claims, reproduced scope, new controls and adoption. These are exposed development artifacts, not fifteen independent confirmations of a learning method.

## The strongest acquisition lead

Return 08 provides a concrete connection between retaining a computation and learning what to ask next. An authored learner acquires continuation tests, basis histories, transition matrices and readouts from the outputs of five programs. Adding a sixth requires a larger interface. The retained continuation questions can reduce the work needed to extract that interface, beyond retaining the previous numerical answers.

The original follow-up mixes several starting conditions. Additional controls match the answer cache and the extra sixth-program anchor explicitly. Every fitted model in the new controls has dimension 16, passes its exact reference certificate and matches all 768 later scalar checks.

| Same starting information within each pair | Generic questions: new teacher executions | Retained questions: new teacher executions |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original 4,243-answer cache; no new sixth-program anchor | 1,650 | 1,198 |
| Original cache; identical extra sixth-program anchor | 2,113 | 1,178 |
| Cache including the failed-readout measurements | 1,605 | 1,133 |

The first matched condition saves 452 executions, about 27.4%. These are executions of frozen teacher programs, not language-model calls. The question directions were acquired; the weighted-linear representation, exact learning algorithm, initial seed and challenge schedule were supplied. The new comparisons are analyses on the same exposed sources, not independent confirmation.

The simpler rivals matter. A family-aware fitter needs no new teacher queries. Separate sparse modules cost 2,466 initial executions, versus 4,243 for the joint extraction. Ordinary factoring matches correctness and serves the short later workloads faster. Initial acquisition plus extension costs 5,421 executions, compared with 5,444 for fresh six-program extraction; that 23-call difference is not a large lifetime advantage, and the two paths have different earlier obligations. Retained questions deserve a broader test, without turning an extension discount into a universal economic claim.

### Six matched orderings

The [continuation-memory follow-up](continuation-memory.md) now checks all six predeclared leave-one-out conditions. Each starts by acquiring five of the same archived programs once, then clones that exact answer cache into both extension arms. Only one arm receives the acquired continuation questions. Neither receives a new sixth-program anchor or the failed-readout observations. Transition matrices are re-extracted; the transferred object is the learned question set.

| Sixth original program | Cache-only extension | Retained-test extension | Calls saved | Cache-only lifetime | Retained-test lifetime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 740 | 463 | 277 | 5,963 | 5,686 |
| 1 | 2,049 | 1,642 | 407 | 5,852 | 5,445 |
| 2 | 1,456 | 708 | 748 | 6,175 | 5,427 |
| 3 | 1,850 | 1,642 | 208 | 5,653 | 5,445 |
| 4 | 1,254 | 463 | 791 | 6,489 | 5,698 |
| 5 | 1,650 | 1,198 | 452 | 5,893 | 5,441 |

Retained questions reduce extension work in every condition, by 208–791 teacher executions. An independent verifier replayed 77,865 newly executed teacher queries, reconstructed paired caches and costs, recomputed all 30 exact certificates, and replayed 18,432 later source-program outputs. It used no result normalization or exclusions. These are six correlated robustness conditions over the same archived programs and authored learning algorithm, with a hypothesis selected after reading the return; they are not six independent source replications or prospective confirmation.

The strongest ordinary control still costs fewer teacher executions: six separately acquired modules total **3,071 calls**, below every joint-memory lifetime in the table. They retain 25 linear coordinates versus 16 jointly; fewer coordinates do not by themselves establish lower memory bytes or better total utility. Cold all-six extraction costs 5,433–5,444 calls. The retained lifetime is lower in only two conditions, by 17 and 3 calls; cold extraction also omits the earlier five-program service obligation. The disclosed-family fitter requires zero new teacher queries from its existing public calibrations, while its fitting computation remains charged separately.

The supported v1 result is that source-dependent continuation questions improve this joint learner’s later extraction beyond answer caching across the tested orderings. It is not overall graph superiority, a better general learning algorithm, production economics or language-model acquisition.

### Transfer into ordinary separate modules

The exploratory v2 follow-up tests a different use of the acquired questions. Both arms acquire an ordinary held-out module from an empty target-answer cache. They share the cost of learning the other five modules. One starts with generic seeds; the other receives every unique continuation suffix acquired from those prior modules. No prior numerical answer becomes target truth. The selection wrapper and learner are authored.

| Held-out original program | Generic target calls | Transferred-suffix target calls | Calls saved | Generic lifetime | Transferred-suffix lifetime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 258 | 380 | −122 | 3,071 | 3,193 |
| 1 | 622 | 671 | −49 | 3,071 | 3,120 |
| 2 | 447 | 574 | −127 | 3,071 | 3,198 |
| 3 | 803 | 768 | 35 | 3,071 | 3,036 |
| 4 | 336 | 477 | −141 | 3,071 | 3,212 |
| 5 | 605 | 671 | −66 | 3,071 | 3,137 |

Transferred suffixes win once and lose five times, using 470 extra target calls across the six conditions. All six outcomes are retained; there is no selection of a winning variant after execution. An independent verifier reproduced all twelve acquisitions, including 6,612 new teacher queries, twelve exact reference certificates and 1,536 later native outputs. Every arm passes its certificate and all 128 later checks. The certificates establish agreement with the declared rational-linear reference over its alphabet, rather than arbitrary external-source validity.

This post-v1 exploratory comparison uses the same six archived programs. It does not invalidate v1’s matched-cache result, but it does not support automatic transfer of the entire question set into ordinary modules. The 3,071-call separate-module control is cheaper than every joint v1 lifetime. Retaining a question and deciding when it is useful are distinct capabilities; useful transfer now needs a selection procedure that earns its cost. Local timings are diagnostics, not speed benchmarks.

Both versions, original protocols, all outcomes and verification sources are available in the [portable evidence archive](../../examples/continuation-memory/leave-one-out-v1/README.md). Its manifest binds 355 archive members; personal source-root metadata is projected out while source bytes and original protocol hashes are preserved. A fresh archive receiver passed all 42 acquisition/certificate replays, 84,477 teacher queries and 19,968 later native outputs. These audit executions are separate from the original study costs.

## Acquired representations should generate challenges

Return 03 makes a useful second connection. Proposed cancellation laws imply experiments which can refute the representation itself. An exceptional source initially induces a false modulus 37 explanation that fits all retained observations. The derived 74-repetition query rejects it. Integer normal forms also preserve periodic distinctions that rational-span closure can erase.

This is bounded representation acquisition and model-derived testing inside a supplied commutative-group language. The ordinary same-evidence integer-library control ties its 1,920 later checks. Detecting order sensitivity does not yet produce a replacement language. The result motivates letting an acquired abstraction propose its own next test, while keeping the native answer independent of its prediction.

Returns 02 and 09 address another obligation: a revised program may work from a fresh start yet be unable to continue a running process. Recovery must preserve the distinctions the new task needs, and a probe may change the state it is trying to observe. Their state, migration and homing constructions are useful references. Their languages, recovery interfaces and several strong fitters are supplied. We defer their runtime migration machinery until a task actually needs continuity; the present native queries start fresh.

## Diagnose the right boundary

The returned SQLite audits clarify three different failures. The original rows-only helper really loses insertion history: two empty tables can require different next identifiers. A deterministic helper receiving only those identical rows cannot supply both answers. By contrast, the selected global and contextual programs retain pending inserts internally but ignore them at their final readouts. An altered readout repairs those cases without new state.

Returns 12,14 and 15 investigate this same archived source defect at different finite depths. Their traces must not be pooled as three independent domains. Return 15 selects a readout with one additional native query per program, but an already-correct archived program is selected with the same query. Return 14's authored prefix generator finds the shared error at its fourth new prefix probe. Archive reuse and competent fixed tests are serious alternatives.

A boundary diagnosis must include all legitimate inputs, state and downstream use. Return 11 adds an important refinement: work backwards through each consumer's acceptable outputs, then check the joint obligations for cases with the same complete node input. Different final answers can accept the same node output, and pairwise-compatible obligations can still have an empty joint intersection. Its finite CEGIS demonstration selects the correct guard from twelve authored rules. The direct ordinary constraint solver solves the same task much more cheaply. We retain the guard as a checked static control, not as a new learned search algorithm.

A model's witness note can propose a missing distinction, a different readout, another observation or removal of an unnecessary decomposition. The note remains a hypothesis. Actual code, source scope and measured outcomes determine what follows. Hashes identify bytes; they do not certify complete context, stable semantics or independence between components.

## What was adopted

The [conformance study](../studies/native/conformance-study.md) implements the small loop using the existing executor, provider admission, shared budget and replay discipline. Its native instruments exercise SQLite allocation, LRU-cache behavior and strict incremental UTF-8 decoding. Fresh model parents remain in the denominator, including correct and technically failed proposals. Ordinary and witness conditions receive the same public evidence and can repair the whole program; a fixed tester supplies a strong cheaper comparison.

An executable investigation policy can use accumulated receipts across several probes without another model call for each question. The host records valid measurements, invalid attempts, duplicates and execution failures. Optional notes accompany that evidence into repair. Candidate selection preserves each previously correct measured parent case. Construction freezes before later panels are opened.

This implements investigation and repair, not demonstrated transfer of an acquired investigation policy. Witness notes are prompt-level requests, not certified fault localization or privileged graph routing. The protocol also gives every arm a repair opportunity even without a detected counterexample, so improvements over the first parent alone cannot identify the value of investigation. The completed native comparison favors fixed testing over either generated policy. Its separate outcomes and costs are in the [current campaign receipt](advance-campaign-20261014.json) and [method](../studies/native/conformance-study.md); the return reproductions are not pooled with those results.

Return 13's source-custody extraction is adopted. [The source-custody owner](../../zenith/source_snapshot.py) builds snapshots without importing the historical financial engine. Existing import paths and broad archive membership remain compatible. Its five code/test changes passed 43 focused checks. The maintained programme and method documents now describe the implemented loop, and the backend/frontend reading-room routes select the current campaign separately from historical evidence. These are adaptations of the proposals; the returned manuscript patch is not itself scientific evidence, and no old scientific bundle is regenerated.

## The next acquired capability

The next useful trace should show experience changing a question or procedure which helps a later externally specified task. Preserve the experimental recipe separately from the repaired answer program. On a new binding, reacquire the answer; do not copy the old verdict. The v2 result makes selection central: retaining all prior questions can increase work. A transfer comparison should remove old solutions, fitted constants and answer tables, match prior experience and total cost, and retain correct/no-change and invalid-reuse cases.

An acquired recipe helping later work is one claim. Better organization than an ordinary code-and-test archive is another. Improvement of the learning policy itself is stronger still. The returns give workable mechanisms and discriminating controls for those questions. They support one evolving loop whose retained computations and experiments earn their use, with freedom to revise the representation when evidence warrants it.
