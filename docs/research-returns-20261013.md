# Ten research returns: what changes the next experiment

The returns give Zenith a concrete failure to investigate: **the programme changes
the meaning of supplied quantities, asks tools questions they do not execute, and
can propose repairs whose promised effect is outside the selected node's available
actions.** The next experiment should test a useful, repeatable correction to that
process. Predicting the already-revealed February decline is the wrong success
condition for a Director-only instruction edit.

This is a Type A synthesis of ten advisory returns received on 13 October 2026,
checked against development source `3e80240b27e91a8e048e9872f5337d6f5b96fa9e`
and the archived executed source `76a4cb1fd028db866a659ed3ff1989179ec9cb20`.
The [verification receipt](research-returns-20261013.json) identifies every ZIP,
its hash, the checks rerun here and the source witnesses. The unsuffixed packet
01 is the original input; packet 01 with `(1)` is the actual return. The other
nine supplied ZIPs are returns.

All ten are offline proposals and prototypes. None contains a new experimental
model campaign. Agreement between reviewers of the same trace is useful for
locating evidence, but is not ten independent demonstrations of a design's value.
This assimilation changes research documentation, not the native runtime.

## Evidence that survived checking

### 1. A meaning error occurs before the final forecast selection

The saved subsystem and interventions readers call trailing returns predictions.
For B04, the observed return of **+11.2472%** ends at the 2 February cutoff;
the **+1.1576%** baseline forecasts the following five sessions. Their difference
does not measure forecast error. The synthesis nevertheless treats it as evidence
of underprediction. Another path turns a twenty-session predictor delay into a
twenty-day forecast, although the numerical target remains five sessions.

The two first-pass readers are parallel error sources. There is no unique global
first node. Their assertions and downstream textual propagation are visible in
[the original trace](../examples/full-graph/trace.json). Whether correcting them
changes the investigation or final selection remains an intervention question.

The practical distinction has three parts: a feature's lookback window, the age
of its observation at forecast time, and the future target horizon. Code can
preserve those definitions and reject a named forecast-error calculation between
mismatched intervals. It cannot certify the truth of every accompanying sentence.
Legitimate descriptive comparisons between past performance and future expectations
must remain possible.

### 2. The numerical catalogue constrains the possible repair

I reran return 10's inspected audit against the original numerical source and
frozen stores. All six original candidate predictions and validation gains matched
exactly in this environment. The exhaustive February catalogue contains 120 legal
source/operation/delay recipes for each of six targets; **all 720 forecasts are
positive**. Some recipes are aliases, so this is not 720 independent experiments.

Director's saved menu is narrower: baseline or the one computed candidate for
each basket, giving 64 complete vectors. The following losses are squared
fractional-return errors, averaged over the six baskets:

| Selection on the exposed February case | MSE |
|---|---:|
| Original selection | 0.0013997010678134024 |
| Hindsight-best selection from the same six candidates and baselines | 0.0012835782353541553 |
| Original selection with only nonpositive-gain Q1 removed | 0.0014054091967514212 |
| Zero-return reference, unavailable to that selector | 0.0006426686619997445 |

Even perfect hindsight selection at that boundary removes only **8.296%** of the
saved loss. That is a conditional limit on a particular menu, not irreducible
market uncertainty. A positive expectation followed by a negative observation is
not, by itself, defective reasoning. The 720-query sweep exceeds the ordinary
six-query allowance and is a diagnostic, not a forecasting arm.

The reviewed instruction to reject nonpositive validation gain really reached
Director: its archived prompt was reconstructed again and its recorded hashes
matched. Executing that instruction changes B01 but worsens this realised outcome.
Keep instruction conformance, behavioural effect and predictive value separate.
Do not promote the demonstration gate as a learned policy.

### 3. An investigation must answer its declared question

In [the numerical owner](../zenith/basket_panel.py), `trend`, `breadth`, `volume`
and `lag` select columns for ridge refits. The query's free-text reason is not
executed. In particular, volume-only versus all-feature prediction is a comparison
of entire recipes; it is not an experiment adding previously absent volume data
or identifying an economic cause. The baseline already contains those columns.

Returns 03 and 08 supply a stronger distinction: **inspect a measured quantity;
then compare a declared forecasting change.** Their B04 example is useful because
23 of 24 constituents rising can coexist with concentrated contribution to the
basket's rise. Count participation and contribution concentration are different
quantities, not mutually exclusive explanations. A mandatory either/or schema
would manufacture a false dichotomy here.

Those returns use different added features and validation designs. Their numerical
effects must not be averaged or treated as replication of the same experiment.
Both preserve the important adverse result: recovering a useful descriptive
distinction does not necessarily justify a forecast adjustment.

I independently recomputed the saved reference losses. Zero beats the all-universe
ridge on the eight February validation origins for every basket; ridge beats zero
for every basket on the August validation origins. Q4 itself beats zero on the
February validation sample. The conclusion is to expose numerical references
fairly, not to install an always-zero forecasting policy.

### 4. A saved repair result is not yet experimental memory

Inspection of `research/graph_failure_study.py`,
`research/refinement_context_check.py` and `research/phase_context_repair_check.py`
supports return 06's narrow finding: the inspected Evolve follow-ups consume the
original subject/Oracle input, without the measured effects of preceding repairs.
The results are saved, but are not a demonstrated input to the next proposal.

The next native Evolve design needs an explicit dependency from a tested edit to
its next input, plus a first-class no-change result. One proposer should submit
the exact edit that the versioner tests. Another model need not paraphrase it.
An instruction with an example, a numerical admission rule and a tool extension
are different edit types and require different execution and replay scopes.

### 5. One small receipt defect is independently confirmed

`investigate` reports the latest label in the complete training store as
`validation_last_label_date`. The actual latest validation labels are:

|---|---|
| 2025-02-02 | 2025-01-23 |
| 2025-05-04 | 2025-04-27 |

This is inaccurate recency metadata, not evidence of future-label leakage in the
audited folds. Correct it in a new numerical receipt version, distinguish training
and validation endpoints, and retain historical bytes. It is captured with the
native continuation work; it has not been repaired by this documentation change.

The prototypes are not interchangeable patches. They disagree about call boundaries,
which decisions models own, the available edit family and the scoring design.
Adopt useful mechanisms into the existing owners after testing them, rather than
merging ten separate frameworks.

## Recommended implementation order

**First establish a competent, measurable task interface.** Preserve the current
runtime and source checkpoints. Add explicit quantity definitions, literal query
semantics, accurately dated numerical receipts and one authoritative action
representation. Keep the shared universe and complete target coverage. These are
engineering changes, with their own unchanged control under the new runtime.

The first small model assay should distinguish the existing view, typed input
alone, typed input plus a temporal rule, and typed input plus that rule and a
worked contrast. Fix model, source, task and budget before execution; retain all
predeclared attempts. Include real matured forecast/outcome comparisons, descriptive
past/future comparisons, and irrelevant identifier/order changes. Grade the
meaning of cited quantities, not whether prose contains a preferred phrase.
The teacher arm tests teachability; it does not count as autonomous discovery.

**Then test consequential use and close the feedback dependency.** Replay the
affected computation against an unchanged arm under the same runtime. Regenerate
numerical results when queries change. The two first-pass error sources and direct
input bypasses make a one-node-only claim insufficient for a shared lesson. The
current local replay API accepts one intervention node; use fresh full Lab runs
until multi-root replay is explicitly implemented and tested.

Require a change in an investigation, interpretation with an operational consumer,
or decision where the repaired distinction matters. Record unchanged and adverse
forecast results. Pass that exact tested-edit receipt into the next Evolve input
and verify its presence. A second, neutral discovery episode must withhold this
review's preferred diagnosis and reference answer from the experimental learner.
The learner may identify another supported failure or decline a repair.

**Compare architectures separately.** The most promising target is one bounded
adaptive investigator, numerical inspect/compare/commit tools, one graph-aware
diagnostic function and exact edit testing. Its execution still produces a graph.
Keep the current graph as a named comparator until the challenger earns the change.
Match accessible evidence, numerical capabilities, model and resource allowance;
do not give the challenger a precomputed clue unavailable to the graph. Include a
deterministic numerical comparator over the same permitted family. A small
enumerable family does not establish a need for language-model reasoning merely
because the underlying dataset is large.

Do not enforce a proposed numerical filter inside an experiment that purports to
measure whether the model follows that filter. Let valid contrary choices remain
visible and score conformance. When a policy is deliberately implemented in code,
credit the resulting constraint to that implementation and evaluate its usefulness
separately. This resolves the apparent conflict between the gate prototypes and
the prompt-compliance prototypes.

## Evidence ceiling and continuation

The next claim to earn is: **a failure teaches a reusable investigation procedure,
which changes an appropriate action on another case while preserving legitimate
behaviour.** Correct prose alone earns a narrower semantic-repair claim. An
engineer-installed fix earns an engineering result. Prediction improvement needs
its own later comparison with numerical changes held common across arms.

All supplied February, May and August outcomes are exposed development evidence.
Simply withholding them until the end of a new script does not recreate blindness.
Freeze the programme and evaluator before separately protected or prospective
evaluation. Treat dates or suitable temporal blocks as replication units; baskets
and overlapping horizons are dependent. Historical data-vintage and possible model
pretraining exposure remain limitations.

Local validation: all 5,532 original manifest entries matched; all ten returned
prototype suites passed, **279 tests total**; six original candidates reproduced
exactly; the 720-forecast catalogue and prompt reconstruction were rerun. The
receipt distinguishes an initial schema-library environment mismatch from the
successful runs in a compatible environment. These tests verify instruments and
arithmetic, not learned behaviour. The native runtime's earlier 392-test result
remains historical; that suite was not rerun for this documentation-only change.
