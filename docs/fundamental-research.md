## What the evidence now supports

The attached A-share files independently reproduce the following date-equal results on 330 cases across 33 already exposed dates:

| Forecast | MSE |
| --- | ---: |
| Zero | 0.0010951782973 |
| Numerical anchor actually present in forward packets | 0.0016647073894 |
| Chronologically scaled numerical control | 0.0010872884797 |
| Same plus chronologically scaled text increment | 0.0010865305952 |

The forward anchor is 52.0% worse than zero. It is a different object from the later ridge control; calling both a numerical baseline obscures what the reasoner would actually see. Text adds a 0.0697% MSE reduction relative to the scaled numerical control. Its common-level improvement is 8.4373e-7 while its relative-stock shape worsens by 8.5842e-8. The text correction changes forecasts on only five dates: 17 and 24 November, and 1, 15 and 22 December 2023. Four improve and one worsens; the other 28 are identical. This is not broad evidence of transferable text reasoning. It also does not measure the unexecuted LLM. [Exact calculations and per-date results](fundamental-research-audit.json).

The strongest reason to move is the mismatch between the evidence and the task: short titles, a weak anchor, and a noisy five-session price target make failures hard to diagnose. A later reported financial quantity provides a more direct check of scope, horizon and business interpretation. A gain there would establish fundamental forecasting ability; market surprise and trading profitability remain separate questions.

## Use the new corpora selectively

The pinned STRUX full release contains 11,411 unique ticker/date records for 858 tickers, spanning 12 January 2017 through 4 September 2024. The 1,100 training and 587 test rows are predominantly overlapping published subsets; one key in each subset is absent from the full split, so they are not perfect partitions. Actual Parquet metadata and ticker/date columns were inspected. The released row has ticker, date, participants, prepared remarks and Q&A; it lacks the source URL, exact publication clock, fiscal-period identity and next-quarter fundamental labels needed here. Its published train/test split is not our experiment split. [Pinned dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BUILDERlym/STRUX-Transcripts/tree/fe0c4139ed3f7bd0024ff39091c7c1fbcd0b15ee); [STRUX paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-short.11/).

A concrete source-assembly starting point exists: AMZN, CRM and FDX each have eight released calls during 2022–2023. This is a 24-call inventory, not 24 admitted forecast/outcome pairs and not a final test set. Adobe has no calls in that slice. Begin by joining a few of these records to official contemporaneous and subsequent releases; admit the full call only after call completion and transcript availability, not at the earlier earnings-release acceptance time; do not build around assumed ticker coverage. Historical transcript redistribution rights and historical byte provenance remain unresolved. Keep raw acquisitions private while using pinned pointers and audited facts in the repository.

EDGAR-Forecast supplies useful parsed filings and selected numerical questions, but its construction sees the target filing when choosing forecastable quantities. The inspected NAPCO cutoff is 1 February 2026 for a period ended 31 December 2025, with the target filed on 2 February. This is a pre-target-filing estimate of a completed period, a different task from forecasting the next business quarter after a call. NAPCO is exposed development. [Pinned release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sfd-anonymous/edgar-forecast-benchmark/tree/6b406d516802d91f2c987390aac837e1a546602f); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18192v2).

Its scorer must be replaced. A reproduction using the pinned source gives zero forecasts **2/5** on the five NAPCO targets: percentage answers generate fractional alternatives, but the absolute tolerance keeps its percentage-point magnitude. Zero is therefore accepted against 0.276 and 0.179 under a 0.5 tolerance. A generic multiple-number grid reused for every question earns 5/5 because the scorer chooses the best matching candidate. These are scorer counterexamples, not a re-evaluation of published model rankings. The declared scalar output must have one finite value, one canonical unit, exact target identity and continuous error; converting a rate must also convert its tolerance. [Source functions, lines and hashes](fundamental-source-audit.json); [pinned scorer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sfd-anonymous/edgar-forecast-benchmark/blob/6b406d516802d91f2c987390aac837e1a546602f/scripts/edgar_forecast_bench.py).

Thus an eight-company paid EDGAR pilot is optional, not the next mandatory stage. We have already learned the main scorer lesson offline. Use a bounded filing case if it exposes a specific extraction/contract failure in our implementation. Do not grow a second benchmark programme before trying the main financial task.

## What the examples teach—and do not teach

For AWS, carrying a rounded 20% growth rate gives $22.1292bn; mapping January's disclosed mid-teens rate to 15% and carrying it forward gives $21.20715bn, against the subsequent $21.354bn. The arithmetic is correct. The CFO's January update belongs to prepared remarks; analyst questions repeat it. It is not full-quarter guidance, and treating one month's run rate as the entire quarter is a persistence assumption. A closer retrospective answer does not validate that assumption generally. The fixed baseline must receive that update too. [Inspected transcript and provenance](fundamental-source-audit.json); [Amazon's subsequent release](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2023/Amazon-com-Announces-First-Quarter-Results/).

Adobe exposes a richer issue. Carrying December's annual range into the March comparison, then subtracting Q1 actual revenue and Q2 guidance, yields an implied second-half arithmetic envelope of **$9.665–9.895bn**. June's annual range minus the first two actual quarters yields **$9.779–9.879bn**. The midpoint rises $49m, but the lower boundary rises $114m and the upper boundary falls $16m. The ranges overlap. These are arithmetic envelopes, not probability intervals: there is no supplied joint distribution between annual and quarterly guidance. Narrowing partly resolves the previously uncertain Q2 amount and cannot automatically be interpreted as greater confidence about H2. December's annual guidance was carried, not reaffirmed in March. [Original source manifest](guidance-development-sources.json); December p.2, March pp.2/4 and June pp.2/4; [reproducible arithmetic](fundamental-research-audit.json).

The useful learned object is consequently a **conditional comparison procedure**: choose a valid reference; preserve period, scope, basis and ranges; remove amounts already realized; identify what remains uncertain; then decide whether a forecast adjustment follows. A generic positive/negative signal would discard precisely the structure that made the example informative.

## A sharper experiment

Fix ordinary issuer revenue as the initial primary target. A stable segment series can be a separately declared target, selected before inspecting forecast errors. SEC companyfacts covers standard whole-entity facts; a segment such as AWS needs its source table or dimensional filing facts. Never assume the aggregate API supplies it. [SEC API contract](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces). Do not choose issuer versus segment after discovering which predicts better. Report GAAP operating profitability separately. Record fiscal start/end, issue cutoff, first outcome publication, exact accession/source and whether the target quarter is partly underway. Scale revenue error by the prior-year counterpart known at issue time; predeclare treatment when a comparable denominator is unavailable.

Construct a shared numerical state with verified quantities, guidance endpoints, latest operating updates and known history. Seasonal persistence, last year-over-year growth, guidance midpoint and disclosed run rate should each remain visible controls. Choose any combined baseline only with matured development outcomes. Issuer-specific guidance-bias calibration must have enough eligible history and a fallback for unseen issuers.

This baseline gives the LLM a serious opponent, but the residual formulation is an attribution device, not a permanent architecture constraint. A strong direct forecast remains useful. The model can explain a proposed adjustment and its evidence; deterministic code handles units and arithmetic. It retains the admitted raw passages so an imperfect extraction cannot become an invisible ceiling.

Separate three comparisons:

1. **Extraction:** raw evidence versus the same evidence with a correct structured numerical state. This measures information handling.
2. **Interpretation:** a strong fixed reasoner versus the numerical controls on identical structured quantities. This asks whether qualifications, timing or business context add value.
3. **Learning:** the same forward system with fixed instructions, retrieved earlier examples, an outcome-trained procedure, or a procedure trained with additional later reporting. This asks whether experience improves interpretation.

Do not turn these into a large factorial study. Use a few development episodes and interventions to identify the active failure, then spend on the comparison that can distinguish it. An accurate extractor is useful even if a residual reasoner never earns an adjustment; that is a different product result from learned forecasting.

Before the four-arm comparison, the fixed system should produce interpretable forecasts on real episodes and show a recurring, forward-detectable error. For example: it repeatedly lets distant pipeline optimism override an in-quarter operating slowdown. The teacher must produce a trigger that could be recognized at the later forecast time. A hindsight statement containing the answer is not such a trigger. A subsequent management narrative is evidence to check, not causal ground truth.

The proposed fixed/memory/outcome/hindsight comparison is sensible, with several qualifications. Match raw evidence, numerical state, teacher capability, proposal opportunities, output limits and fallback support. Memory must not displace forward evidence. Outcome-only feedback gets the complete forward trace, exact outcome, baseline residual and mechanical diagnostics. Outcomes and selection feedback must mature before the first assessment issue cutoff, including on disjoint issuers. All four arms remain frozen after selection.

One learned procedure in each arm supports a comparison of those particular procedures. It does not establish the general superiority of a learning algorithm over its random proposal process. If results warrant it, teacher replication is more informative than presenting 24 correlated assessment episodes as a decisive population result. Use paired issuer/event results and common-period dependence; do not pool revenue and margin as independent demonstrations.

Historical separation also does not remove pretrained-model memory of 2023–2024 outcomes. Date/issuer masking and controlled evidence changes can probe dependence, but cannot certify absence of contamination. A successful historical development result should nominate a prospective frozen continuation, not be renamed a prospective result.

Use the following sequence, without treating the amounts as fresh money:

1. **Offline assembly now:** admit several ordinary disclosure episodes, compute strong baselines, and verify source/period/unit joins. Include the exposed Amazon/Adobe examples as checks, never assessment. Build the future selection/assessment eligibility list without looking at its labels.
2. **First model tranche, at most $0.25 of the existing envelope:** run a native compatibility pilot and a handful of fixed/direct disclosure forecasts. Inspect failures and a few unit/scope/counterfactual probes. If source extraction is the failure, fix it; if explicit numbers explain the gain, retain the extraction result. Do not spend the four-arm budget automatically.
3. **Conditional learning tranche:** when a repeated error has a plausible forward trigger, train matched outcome/hindsight procedures and compare them with fixed reasoning and memory on a small later selection set. Spend only after exact request bounds fit. Keep the simpler system if the change provides no useful evidence.
4. **Assessment reserve:** preserve roughly half the envelope until the above decision. Select counts from the admitted panel and measured request costs, then freeze. Twenty-four episodes are a proposed size, not a power guarantee. If the budget cannot support the declared comparison, narrow the claim and design before running it.

Reuse the native provider/validation/budget and reflection machinery. Adapt source admission from `benchmarks/guidance_events.py`; retain `research/ashare_study.py` as the audited price experiment. A fundamental target needs a distinct typed contract and evaluator rather than silently storing revenue in an expected-return field. No new optimizer is justified by this return. The decision criterion is practical: does experience produce a procedure that improves later financial judgments beyond correctly extracted quantities, a competent fixed reasoner and retrieved examples? That would be the question for this alternative if selected later. The paper can then claim the achieved layer—extraction, fixed forecasting, or transfer from experience—supported by its actual measurements.
