# Learning from a day without a supported forecast

Lab may return an explicit `ABSTAIN` when the supplied evidence does not support
a forward thesis. It must still name and cite observed facts, explain what is
unsupported or missing, and specify evidence that would justify reconsideration.
The usual `FORECAST` branch keeps its direction, price, citation and horizon
requirements. An empty forecast is invalid unless it is an explicit abstention.

CP1 locks the decision mode. Directed miners inspect the available lanes and
retain support limits; CP2 cannot turn an abstained CP1 into a forecast. CP2
preserves the cited explanation and has `predictions_t=[]`. Integrity evaluates
the reasoning behind the no-call. A coherent abstention is not a successful
price prediction, proof of optimality, or proof that no usable signal exists.

Oracle records `ABSTAINED` reconciliation with zero prediction rows and a null
hit rate. Observed prices may still be compared. An unexplained realized move
can be `UNRESOLVED`: its sign does not identify a cause, novelty or remaining
pricing. Oracle can report `NO_FINDINGS` with an evidence-bound review when it
cannot establish an earlier error. Ordinary critiques still require concrete
errors and references. Evolve can retain the current policy with `no_change`.

A cohort retains an abstained run as a terminal attempt. It does not silently
resample that date. Abstention counts as missing forecast coverage, with no
zero-loss credit and no score or winner on incomplete support. The present
generation gate therefore cannot select an abstaining strategy. A later
selective-forecast experiment would need its own predeclared coverage and
utility objective; silently dropping no-calls would reward avoidance.

## Development evidence

The July 1–2, 2020 InvestorBench pilot exposed three forecast-only assumptions:
Lab had to select a dominant cause, Oracle had to classify realized moves as
priced or surprising, and critique had to identify an error. Explicit no-call,
unresolved and no-findings branches address those assumptions in the original
engine. Historical attempts and their source identities remain intact.

This packet contained 73 earlier news summaries and twenty scalar prices per
symbol. Its positive sentiment labels often accompanied third-party products,
general market estimates or historical associations. Two records described the
same Microsoft skills initiative; Honeywell's installed base was not a new
order count; JNJ's indication-specific trial stop did not establish an existing
product safety problem. These distinctions were examined before later prices
were opened. They are source-reading observations, not return predictors.

The later snapshot contained eleven newly admitted summaries. Two described the
same 3Cloud financing; a report of declining Mixer viewership and closure still
carried a positive label. The three scalar prices rose, but those observations
did not establish a supported earlier directional call or its causal explanation.
A sentiment label must remain a source assertion, separate from issuer exposure,
change, novelty and measured magnitude. The [pinned adapter](investorbench.md)
records the corpus and clock limitations.

The [execution receipt](abstention-receipt.json) binds the completed development
run, retained predecessors, response reuse and reviewed Evolve decision. Reusing
previously authored answers to exercise amended contracts is replay evidence;
it is not a fresh model sample or an untouched historical forecast. No paid
inference or demonstrated learning gain is claimed.
