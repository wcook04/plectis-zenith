Those attempts reported $0.05197758 in charges and used $0.5856564 of reservations. A reservation is an upper bound, not an invoice. No screen or confirmation panel had opened.

The dataset, archived runtime, evaluator, model, pinned routes, reasoning settings, arm order, probe limits, call ceiling and decision rules remained those of the [prospectively frozen v5 protocol](../../progress-protocol-v5-20261014.md). Its [public partial export](../../../examples/conformance/progress-diagnostic-v5-reservation-stop/manifest.json) is audit evidence; it contributes no separate diagnostic estimate or additional charge to the report.

The continuation reuses each of the 40 settled responses by its exact provider call identity. It pays only for previously unattempted model nodes. Reconstructing the continuation reexecutes the deterministic native measurements; the prior model responses are reused from the settled ledger. Offline validation also reexecutes measurements. The published native probe and work counters describe the frozen logical arm trajectories and exclude these reconstruction and audit reexecutions; they do not measure total physical executions, CPU use or wall-clock lifecycle work. Provider attempt and known-charge accounting uses the exact settled identities. No failed or unknown-charge attempt is eligible for continuation, and no model output is repaired or retried. The [machine amendment](progress-v5-funding-amendment-20261015.json) binds the original and amended protocol hashes, the unopened partial replay, the previous paid identities and the original file hashes. The [campaign report builder](../../../scripts/build_progress_report.py) checks that the sole protocol difference is the financial ceiling and that every previous prompt, response and settled billing entry survives unchanged.

This administrative amendment supplies enough reservation capacity to execute the declared comparison. It does not create a new source family, an additional independent replication or a positive research result.
