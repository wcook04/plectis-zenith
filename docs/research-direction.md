# Research direction

The active direction is maintained in [the research programme](../program.md) and [three-page paper](../paper/zenith.pdf): executable observation models, native and explicitly synthetic measurements, and tested revision with supported answers and unresolved goals.

[Executable method](executable-investigations.md) describes the equal-tool comparison against an executable archive and fresh compiler. [Measured status](status.md) and source-bound receipts distinguish interface repairs, compilation, revision and further-case reuse. The current support family is disclosed; source settings are inferred from observations. The [new structural and rule-induction studies](decomposition-20261014.md) acquire useful predictors but do not show added value from repeated revision. Compact streaming state and model-derived investigation policies are the next tests in the programme.

The financial graph and earlier controller proposals are historical work, with no separate active roadmap. PAL, Voyager and DreamCoder establish relevant prior art; program execution and skill libraries are not claimed as new.
