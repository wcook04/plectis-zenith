# Research direction

The active direction is maintained in [the research programme](../program.md) and [current paper source](../paper/zenith.tex): retain executable programs together with useful experiments, then measure whether that experience improves later investigation.

[Native conformance](conformance-study.md) compares model-generated investigators, optional obligation reports and fixed testing before whole-program repair. [Continuation memory](continuation-memory.md) isolates learned test directions from cached answers and accounts for acquisition cost. [Measured status](status.md) and source-bound receipts distinguish delivery, repair and useful reuse. The [fifteen-return synthesis](advance-returns-20261014.md) records adoption and exact reproduction boundaries. The earlier [structural and rule-induction studies](decomposition-20261014.md) retain their findings without claiming that repeated revision helped already-correct first proposals.

The financial graph and earlier controller proposals are historical work, with no separate active roadmap. PAL, Voyager and DreamCoder establish relevant prior art; program execution and skill libraries are not claimed as new.
