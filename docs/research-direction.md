# Research direction

The active direction is maintained in [the research programme](../program.md) and [three-page paper](../paper/zenith.pdf): a competent adaptive investigator, a graph-aware reviewer, and a measured test of one reusable procedure change.

The earlier context-conditioned neural prompt selector was an unimplemented proposal. It is superseded by the smaller executable loop and remains in Git history. There is no separate active controller roadmap.

[Native method](investigator.md) describes what runs. [Execution status](status.md) records what its experiments establish. DSPy, MIPRO and TextGrad are credited in the paper; graph-based feedback and prompt optimisation are not claimed as new.

The next research round must select a useful decision with sufficient available information, then test whether a supported repair transfers. The [modern-model repair report](model-repair-20261013.md) supplies the current evidence and separates component calibration from unsolved review and forecasting. A larger architecture or more ambitious task remains possible when supported by a decisive experiment.
