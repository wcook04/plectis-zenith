# Research notice

**This is not financial advice.** Zenith is an experimental research prototype
for studying how a graph of reasoning steps can be inspected and revised.
Its financial examples illustrate that research question. They are not an
investment service, a trading strategy offered for use, or a recommendation
to buy, sell or hold any asset.

This notice covers the repository, README, paper, diagrams, website and graph
viewer, demonstrations, scripts, historical examples, and model-generated
analysis, selections, rankings, probabilities and forecasts. Words such as
“Decide,” “prediction,” “target,” or “opportunity” in a saved node instruction
or response describe the experiment; they are not instructions to a reader
to transact. A model output can sound confident and still be wrong.

The material is for research and explanation only. It does not consider your
financial circumstances, objectives or tolerance for loss. It is not financial,
investment, tax or legal advice, an offer, or a solicitation to transact.
Do not use the material or outputs to make financial decisions, allocate
capital or execute trades. Seek independent advice from an appropriately
qualified professional for decisions about your own circumstances.

## What the evidence supports

The current demonstration uses real saved stock, ETF and prediction-market
inputs, labelled synthetic macro/news replacements and recomputed calculator
summaries. The reduced graph loads these inputs and prepares its first reasoning
request. It has made no model calls or completed forecasts. This preparation is
not a financial backtest or evidence of prediction improvement. See the
[current receipt](docs/status.md) and [per-feed boundaries](docs/data.md).

Historical examples, simulated results and model outputs do not guarantee
future performance. Saved collection clocks do not verify every underlying
observation's availability, and a current model may know later historical events.
Results from other configurations must not be relabelled as this edition's results.

## Publishing and reuse

Keep the research purpose and this explicit short notice visible wherever a
reader can encounter the material on its own, including a video excerpt,
screenshot, embedded viewer, or page showing model outputs:

> **This is not financial advice.** Zenith is an experimental research
> prototype. Historical examples and model outputs are not investment
> recommendations and must not be used to make financial decisions.

Link to this notice for detail. Keep the measured result and its limitations
beside performance claims. Do not describe the two successful intermediate
outputs as better forecasts, a trading win rate, or proven self-improvement.
Retain notices in exported paper pages and clips; a link or footer alone may
be missed when material is excerpted.
