# Research boundary

Zenith is a public research draft. Current experiments measure model-generated tests and repairs against authored native SQLite, LRU and UTF8 contracts; compare reusable investigators on authored SQLite client workflows; and inspect a continuation learner on eight native configurations. A separate earlier memory study uses six previously acquired programs from one disclosed synthetic source family. Native observations, program-teacher outputs and exact agreement with a supplied algebraic reference are different evidence classes. Repeated parents, related client variants, query rows and leave-one-out orderings are not independent domains or evidence of reliable production operation, open-world discovery or general intelligence. Earlier observation-learning and operational campaigns retain their original source contracts and identification boundaries.

A proposed procedure and its explanation remain hypotheses until tested. Preserve failed attempts, numerical errors, unresolved conditions, complete comparison results and costs. Inspected evaluation cases become development evidence for later engineering decisions. Record a new protocol and fresh cases after changing the runtime or interface.

**This is not financial advice.** The retained financial studies use published historical observations and constructed research indices. [Data provenance](docs/data.md) and [measured status](docs/status.md) preserve their boundaries. Retrospective results do not establish trading profitability or market causes; adjustment vintages, stale quotes, small samples and possible pretraining exposure limit interpretation.

The older System A example is a disclosed synthetic protocol fixture. Historical observations are never relabelled as synthetic. Credentials and private provider transport records remain outside publication.

The separate `offline-example` command completes an authored synthetic Lab/Oracle teaching fixture without network access or model inference. Its fictional targets demonstrate executable contracts, not forecast quality or financial value.
